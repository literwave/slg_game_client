# 关城与军务界面接入

## 关隘

普通要塞保留原素材和 138 的显示宽度。战役关隘使用岩壁山口素材 `assets/resources/ui/world/mountain_pass_saddle.png`，宽度按等级分为 460、540、640；8 级函谷关使用 640。主体由中央关楼、短墙、两侧灰岩与穿门道路组成，取代此前横跨山体的长直城墙。

`GateGeometry.ts` 集中定义素材名、尺寸、锚点、镜像朝向和实体命中轮廓。锚点位于门洞地面，完整画布显示，避免裁边使位置漂移。地图先命中中央关楼和短墙，再处理后方山脉；两侧岩壁、延伸道路和透明角落不属于建筑命中区。选中框宽度为整体素材的 70%，信息框按完整素材高度避让。镜像朝向取环形山脉切线的等距投影方向，函谷关门前道路向右下方穿出山口。

这是美术和点击范围调整。地图坐标、通路、关隘归属、两州通行条件、服务端判定数据没有扩大。

## 任务、邮件、排行

地图“更多”新增三个入口，沿用墨色面板与金线按钮。横竖屏使用安全区域和独立滚动裁切节点；列表操作按钮至少 44 高。新增节点在运行时由脚本建立，没有重写既有场景或预制体。

| 入口 | 已连接的操作 |
|---|---|
| 任务 | 主线、日常、周常分类；配置名称、描述、目标、进度、奖励预览；按服务端 state 领取；单任务防重请求 |
| 邮件 | 系统、联盟、公告；列表分页、详情、附件、读取状态、锁定/解锁、领奖、二次确认删除；有未领取附件或已锁定时禁止删除 |
| 排行 | 战力、主城等级；打开时请求快照、每页 20 条、上一页/下一页、自己的排名和分值 |

请求中的按钮禁用；超时解除请求锁并允许手动重试。未加载、加载中和服务器返回空列表分别显示。领取后的状态等待 ACK，实际奖励仍由既有 Reward 和道具同步协议处理。邮件实例 ID 和排行榜分值保留 int64 字符串精度。

活动按用户要求暂不接入：没有活动 UI、入口或兑换/领奖入口，登录后不主动拉取活动。此前已有的活动协议与数据接口保留，未扩展活动玩法。

使用内置 `image_gen` 生成透明图片，通过 `image_editor_process` 处理尺寸、`image_editor_import` 导入、`image_editor_configure_asset` 设置线性过滤与 clamp。保留原普通要塞美术。

Three chunky rough-hewn pale limestone blocks and a small stone chip in a compact low isometric stack. Warm grey/beige stone, subtle angular cracks, sunlit cream upper faces and deep brown-grey shadows, readable confident dark contour. Match classic Chinese historical strategy game hand-painted resource icons: clean painterly edges, realistic textured materials but simplified readable shapes, no glossy cartoon look. The existing iron resource icon is two dark steel ingots in an isometric pile with warm cream highlights and brown outlines. This is STONE masonry resource, clearly different from black iron ingots. Centered object with 10 percent transparent padding, all pixels outside the small natural contact shadow fully transparent. No text, no numerals, no frame, no badge, no background, no sparkles.

关城提示词：

> Generate a finished transparent-background 2D sprite for a Chinese ancient historical isometric strategy game's monumental Hangu Pass. A formidable broad Qin-Han stone gate fortress spans a narrow mountain road: a massive central dark arched tunnel visibly open through the stone wall, two strong square stone bastions, restrained timber upper gatehouse with grey-brown tiled roof and a few small muted red pennants. Long thick stone wall shoulders extend to the left and right, intentionally designed to join mountains that the game draws separately. Low isometric camera, 2:1 ground plane, view front and right sides, no perspective vanishing point. Wide roughly 1.9:1 silhouette including walls, central gatehouse tall enough to be impressive. Restrained realistic old-school SLG painterly sprite with finely detailed tan-grey masonry, weathered battlements, warm sunlight from upper left, natural olive-brown dirt foundation beneath building only. Monumental military architecture, no gold palace, no pagoda spires, no decorative UI. Centered entire structure with 6 percent transparent padding. Transparent background, no mountains, no sky, no scenery beyond foundation, no people, no text or labels, no border. Must read as a strategic mountain pass sealing a valley rather than a small wooden fort. Produce high resolution around 1536px wide suitable for rendering at 600 game units.

以上关城提示词是此前长城墙版本的生成记录。当前项目改用 1024×608 的岩壁山口版本；完整生成提示词、参考素材、加工参数和文件校验值记录在 `关隘山口美术来源.json`。图片经 AssetDB 导入、线性过滤、clamp、mipmap 与锚点配置读回。旧立绘留存，当前渲染与弹窗均引用新素材。
