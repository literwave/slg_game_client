# 地图收藏标记 v2

由内置 imagegen 生成独立透明原画；经 Cocos Image Core 去透明边、等比缩至 256 像素高并加 4 像素透明边，最终 79 × 264。地图使用 52 像素基准高度，线性过滤与 mipmap。原图不覆盖。

资源：`assets/resources/ui/world/map-bookmark-v2.png`

## 提示词

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original transparent PNG game sprite for a personal bookmarked location on a Chinese Three Kingdoms strategy WORLD MAP, not an army unit.
Primary request: a beautifully hand-painted small planted wayfinding standard, matching a refined realistic isometric ancient Chinese game map. One slim upright dark wooden pole capped with a restrained bronze spear finial. A rich dark vermilion cloth banner hangs from a short crossbar near the top, clear elegant swallowtail cut, bold but restrained antique gold edging, a single simple gold diamond medallion motif in the middle, absolutely no letters or writing. A few visible folds with convincing shaded fabric, a modest binding cord. Pole meets a tiny low bronze ground socket with a soft very small contact shadow, no large plinth or rock terrain.
Style/medium: polished 2.5D hand-painted game art, crisp strong silhouette, tangible cloth, wood and aged bronze, realistic proportions simplified to remain beautiful at only 44 pixels tall. Vertical pole is straight, no dutch tilt. Viewed slightly from above at the same 2:1 isometric angle as a world map, but the banner face is readable. Light from upper left, warm muted earth palette, restrained highlights.
Composition: ONE complete standalone object centered, generous 8 percent transparent margin, full finial, cloth, pole and base visible, fills about 82 percent of canvas height and 50 percent width. No backdrop. Real alpha transparency all around the object. Canvas portrait if possible.
Avoid: text, Chinese characters, numbers, labels, UI plates, buttons, map tiles, landscape, soldiers, horses, armor, large military regiment flag, multiple objects, variants, contact sheet, cartoon vector flat shapes, neon glow, particles, oversharp sparkles, black/white opaque background.
